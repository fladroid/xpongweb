This page — RL 1

The first RL page. The right paddle is an agent that plays but does not learn — a random walker, the zero-baseline of reinforcement learning: each step it moves up, stays, or moves down at random, with no memory and no goal. Use the switch to make the left paddle a human (W/S) or a second agent. The Beam and Heatmap below read the world — the ball's path and past goals — not the agent's mind: there is no mind here yet to X-ray. A learning agent, and the telemetry of its decisions, arrives on the next page.

Beam

The beam shows where the ball is going by pure physics — up to the first wall, paddle or goal. It stops where certainty ends: it never predicts the bounce or anyone's move.

Heatmap

The heatmap is a shadow, not a mind: it records only where the ball has physically crossed each goal line. Thin frames mark the bands on each wall; a band grows darker the more goals have landed in it. Counts accumulate through the session — Reset clears them. Toggle it with the Heatmap switch or the h key.

Navigation

Left player (human mode): W up, S down, or drag on the left half on touch. The right player is the agent. Flip the left paddle to an agent with the switch by the controls.
